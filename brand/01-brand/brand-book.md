# Zorya — brand book

Zorya is a signal-fusion warning app for Poland: it collects official alerts (RCB, IMGW, PSP), siren and acoustic sensors, radio and air-traffic monitoring (SDR, ADS-B) and neighbours' reports, fuses them into one level for a commune, and says what to do. It runs on its own server at zorya.website. Version 1.0 of the identity, September 2026.

## 1. The idea

In Slavic myth the Zorya are the sisters who keep the dawn and the dusk — the morning star that stands watch before the sun is up. The app does the same job: it watches through the night and tells you when something is coming and when it has passed. The Polish descriptor is **czuwanie świtu**, "the dawn watch".

Everything in the identity comes from one picture: **a star over a horizon.** The star is the watcher; the horizon is the line between what is confirmed and what is still only a signal. That picture is the mark, the app's home screen, the layout device of every list, and the way the app grades certainty.

Three words to test any decision against: **quiet, exact, awake.** Quiet — the app is calm by default, a dark sky with one star; nothing shouts until something is real. Exact — every number has a source and a timestamp, every level has a definition, nothing is rounded to look better. Awake — when the level rises, the change is unmistakable: the horizon thickens, the card inverts, the star breathes.

What it is not: a startup dashboard, a weather widget, a military console, a news feed. No gradients, no glow, no shields, no radar sweeps, no exclamation marks.

## 2. Name and wording

- **Zorya** — always with a capital Z in text, ZORYA in the wordmark. Never "Zorja", never "ZORYA" in running text.
- Descriptor: **czuwanie świtu** (lower case). English: *the dawn watch*. Used under the wordmark, in the footer and in meta descriptions; not in headlines.
- Domain: zorya.website, written in lower case without "www".
- The five level words are product vocabulary and are always written in lower case in running text: cisza, obserwacja, ostrzeżenie, alarm, odwołanie. As a heading or a badge they are uppercase (the `label` style) or capitalised (the status word on the home screen).

## 3. The mark

A filled circle (the star) above a horizontal rule (the horizon). Drawn from one unit, **u**: the horizon is 8u long and 0.5u thick; the star is 2u across, centred 6u along the horizon (to the east, where the morning star rises) and 1u above it. The mark's box is 8u × 3.5u. The horizon's bottom edge is the wordmark's baseline. Files under `03-logo/`, all generated by `tools/build-logo.py` from those numbers.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `zorya-mark-night.svg` | The mark on dark grounds: ivory horizon, dawn star. Default on the website and in the app. |
| `zorya-mark-day.svg` | On light grounds: night horizon, dawn-deep star. |
| `zorya-mark-mono.svg` | Single colour, `currentColor` — for the app header, print, embroidery, anywhere the mark takes the text colour. |
| `zorya-wordmark-*.svg` | ZORYA alone, in curves. |
| `zorya-lockup-h-*.svg` | Mark + wordmark, horizontal. The primary lockup: website header, documents, partner material. |
| `zorya-lockup-h-tagline-*.svg` | The same with *czuwanie świtu* under the wordmark: footer, OG image, splash. |
| `zorya-lockup-v-*.svg` | Mark over the wordmark, centred: app store listing, square placements. |
| `level-{cisza,obserwacja,ostrzezenie,alarm,odwolanie}.svg` | The status glyphs for the UI, `currentColor`, 32 × 14. |
| `app-icon-*.png`, `app-icon.svg`, `app-icon-maskable-512.png` | Night square, mark at 62 % width. Square corners — the OS masks them. |
| `favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-16.png` | The mark filling the tile; at 16 px the star is 4 px and the horizon 1 px. |

**Clear space:** 1u on every side of the mark's box (the SVG files include it). Nothing enters it — no text, no edge, no other logo.

**Minimum sizes:** mark 16 px on screen (use the favicon crop below 24 px), 6 mm in print; horizontal lockup 120 px / 30 mm; vertical lockup 96 px / 24 mm.

**Colour of the mark:** the star is always dawn (`dawn` on dark, `dawn-deep` on light); the horizon takes the text colour of its ground. In the app the star's colour is *live*: it carries the current level (see 5). On the website and in static material it is always dawn.

**Never:** rotate it (the horizon is horizontal, always); stretch it; add a third element (rays, a second star, a sun); put it in a circle, a shield or a rounded tile; add a shadow, glow or gradient; recolour it outside the palette; make the star hollow outside the cisza level; place it on a photograph without a solid night or fog panel behind it.

## 4. Colour

Flat colour only. Two themes: **noc** (night, the default — the app is used in the dark) and **dzień** (day). Every value is in `02-tokens/tokens.json`; every text/ground pair is computed in `02-tokens/contrast.json` and passes WCAG AA (most AAA).

| Token | Value | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `night` | #0E1219 | The night sky. Dark ground; the light theme's ink; text on bright status fills. |
| `night-raised` | #161C26 | Dark cards, sheets, header. |
| `night-line` / `night-line-strong` | #2A3341 / #5C6878 | Dark dividers (decorative) / borders that mean something (3.3:1). |
| `ivory` | #F3EDE0 | The star's white. Dark-theme text; the horizon in the mark. Never pure white. |
| `ash` | #A9B0BA | Dark-theme secondary text. |
| `fog` / `fog-raised` | #EEF1F5 / #FFFFFF | Light ground / light cards. |
| `fog-line` / `fog-line-strong` | #CDD4DC / #7D8794 | Light dividers / meaningful borders (3.2:1). |
| `slate` | #545E6B | Light-theme secondary text. |
| `dawn` / `dawn-deep` | #F2B544 / #7D4F00 | Dawn gold: the accent, the star, level obserwacja. Bright on dark, deep on light. |
| `ember` / `ember-deep` | #F0783A / #A83A10 | Level ostrzeżenie. |
| `alarm` / `alarm-deep` | #FF6157 / #B5241C | Level alarm. |
| `calm` / `calm-deep` | #8FC7EA / #1C5A83 | Level odwołanie and links. Blue, not green, so alarm and all-clear never depend on telling red from green. |

Rules:

- Build UI with the **semantic** tokens (`bg`, `bg-raised`, `line`, `line-strong`, `text`, `text-2`, `status-*`, `fill-*`, `on-status`, `link`, `focus`), never the core names — the semantic ones flip with the theme.
- Dawn is the only accent and it is spent on three things: the star, links' underlines and the focus ring. It is not a button colour, not a heading colour, not a decoration.
- Status colours mean levels and nothing else. A chart line, a chip, a toggle never borrows ember or alarm.
- The bright status colours (`dawn`, `ember`, `alarm`, `calm`) are dark-theme colours: on a light ground they fail as graphics, so the light theme uses the `*-deep` versions for text, icons **and fills**, with `ivory` text on the fills.
- A level is never colour alone: it always carries its word and its glyph shape (5). Ember and alarm are close in lightness on purpose — the shape and the inverted card do the work.
- Grounds are flat. No gradients, no dawn-coloured sky, no noise texture. The night is #0E1219 and that is the whole sky.

## 5. The five levels — the brand's own graphic system

The status glyph is the mark itself, changing shape with the level. Word first, then shape, then colour.

| Level | Word (PL) | Glyph | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | cisza | hollow star (2 px ring) over a single horizon | `status-cisza` (ash / slate) | Nothing active. All sources reporting. |
| 1 | obserwacja | filled star over a single horizon | dawn | One signal, or an official notice without local confirmation. Read it. |
| 2 | ostrzeżenie | filled star over a **double** horizon | ember | Two independent sources agree, or an official warning for your area. Prepare. |
| 3 | alarm | filled star over a double horizon; the card **inverts** (alarm fill, night text) | alarm | Official alarm, or a siren confirmed by a second source. Act. |
| 4 | odwołanie | filled star over a single horizon | calm | The event ended. Shown for 60 minutes, then back to cisza. |

The same idea grades **confidence** of an item by the rule under it, like a certainty ladder: solid 2 px = potwierdzone (two independent sources, one official or a sensor); solid 1 px = prawdopodobne; dashed 1 px = pojedynczy sygnał; dotted 1 px = niezweryfikowane (a user report nobody confirmed). Items with a dashed or dotted rule live **below the horizon** in the feed.

The horizon rule itself is a UI element: the bottom edge of the app header (2 px, in the current level's colour), the line between events and raw signals on the home screen, the 2 px rule between sections on the website.

## 6. Typography

One family for everything: **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** (SIL OFL 1.1, variable 200–800), chosen because it was designed for low-vision readers — unambiguous 1/l/I and 0/O, open apertures — and a warning app has to be read at 3 a.m. by someone who just woke up. Its sibling **Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono** sets timestamps, coordinates, frequencies and IDs, and the descriptor under the wordmark. Files in `05-fonts/`; load both the `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets — the Polish glyphs are in `latin-ext`.

| Style | Size / line | Weight | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `display` | 40/44 (56/60 from 900 px) | 800 | Landing hero only |
| `h1` | 28/34 | 700 | Screen titles, event title, the status word |
| `h2` | 22/28 | 700 | Section headings |
| `h3` | 18/24 | 700 | Card titles |
| `body` | 17/26 | 400 | Running text. Never under 16 px on screen |
| `body-strong` | 17/26 | 700 | The level word inside a sentence |
| `small` | 14/20 | 400 | Helper text, captions, legend — the app's floor |
| `label` | 13/18, +0.08 em, uppercase | 500 | Badges, eyebrows, chips |
| `mono` / `mono-small` | 15/22 · 13/18 | 500 | Times, dates, coordinates, IDs |
| `wordmark` | — | 800, +0.08 em, uppercase | ZORYA; live type only in the app header at 18 px |

Rules: weights 400, 500, 700, 800 only; no italic in the UI (italic exists for quotations on the website); headings are sentence case; uppercase only in `label` and the wordmark. Times are 24-hour `05:41`, dates ISO `2026-09-20` in mono; in a sentence `20 września` is fine. Decimal comma, thin no-break space for thousands (`2 400 MHz`), `−` (U+2212) for minus, Polish quotation marks „ ”. Units with a space: `300 m AGL`, `96 dB`, `868 MHz`.

## 7. Layout and surfaces

- **Square corners everywhere** (`radius.0`). The only circles are the star and the dot in a toggle.
- **No shadows.** Elevation is a 1 px `line-strong` border on a `bg-raised` ground.
- **One column** in the app, 40 rem max; the map is full-bleed. The website is a 72 rem column with a 64 ch measure for text; sections are separated by 2 px horizon rules, not by background colour changes.
- Spacing on a 4 px base (`space.1`–`space.20`); list rows at least 56 px; tap targets at least 44 px.
- Cards do not get a coloured left border. A level card has a full 1 px border in the level colour; the alarm card inverts.
- Density: the home screen shows the status, then events, then the horizon, then signals — in that order, always. Nothing is above the status block.

## 8. Motion

Almost none. Hover and press feedback at 120 ms, sheets at 200 ms, one easing (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`). The only expressive motion is the **alarm star breathing** — opacity 100 → 60 % over 2 s, ease-in-out, only at level alarm and only while the alarm is unread. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves; the word carries it. No skeleton shimmer, no parallax, no animated horizon.

## 9. Iconography and imagery

- Icons: a small set of line icons at 20 px, 1.5 px stroke, square caps and mitre joins, `currentColor`. The set is closed: horyzont (the mark), mapa, sygnały, zgłoś, więcej, obszar (pin), źródło, czas, udostępnij, wycisz, ustawienia, powrót, zamknij, filtr, dodaj plik. Draw or pick them (Lucide at 1.5 px is an acceptable base) but keep the square-cap rule. No filled icons, no emoji, no coloured icons.
- The status glyphs are not icons — they are the mark, and only they may be coloured.
- Imagery: none. No stock photos, no illustrations of sirens or drones, no hero renders. The website's pictures are the app itself (a real screen in a bordered panel) and the horizon diagram. A map, when shown, follows the app's map tokens: land is the page ground, water `map-water`, roads `map-roads`, boundaries dashed `map-boundary`, an event area in its status colour at `map-area-alpha` with a 1.5 px outline.

## 10. Voice in one paragraph

Short. Concrete. Polish, second person singular, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no reassurance that is not a fact. A level word, then what, then where, then since when, then the source. When the app does not know, it says so: *brak potwierdzenia*, *pojedynczy sygnał*, *niezweryfikowane*. Full rules and every UI string: `01-brand/voice-and-copy.md`, `04-ux/ui-strings.pl.json`.

## 11. Co-branding

Zorya is the product's own identity. If it appears beside the maker's marks (the *Matthew Halaba* footer logo or the *Fundacja Terra Incognita* lockup), it goes in the footer at the same cap height as those marks, separated by a 1 px `line-strong` vertical rule, on Zorya's own ground. Never merge the marks or share a lockup.
